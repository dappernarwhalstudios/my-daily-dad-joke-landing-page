# Privacy Policy

At *My Daily Weather*, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and your rights regarding it. By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined below.

<br>

## 1.0 Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. We do not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers directly, and there is no account or sign-in of any kind.

### 1.1 Location

If you grant location permission, your device's location is used only to fetch the current forecast for "Current Location" — it is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve weather data and is not stored on our servers or shared with anyone else. You can add and view forecasts for other cities without ever granting location access, and you can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Basic technical details such as device type, operating system version, and app version.
- **Usage Data:** Anonymous usage statistics such as which theme or units you use, and whether widgets are added.

### 1.3 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Apple WeatherKit | Provides forecast data for the location you request. |
| Firebase Analytics | Provides anonymized usage metrics to help us improve the app. |

We do not use advertising or ad-tracking SDKs in this app. Please review these services' own privacy policies for more information.

<br>

## 2.0 How We Use the Information

The limited data we collect is used to maintain and improve app functionality, and to fix bugs. We do **not** sell your data or use it for any kind of profiling or advertising.

<br>

## 3.0 Purchases

The one-time Premium unlock and optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information.

<br>

## 4.0 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access the data we have about you (if any) or request its deletion. You can disable location access at any time in iOS Settings.

<br>

## 5.0 Children's Privacy

*My Daily Weather* is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe this has happened, please contact us immediately.

<br>

## 6.0 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.
