### What you get

- Every temperature shown in both Celsius and Fahrenheit, everywhere in the app
- Current conditions, an hourly outlook, and a 10-day forecast
- Wind, humidity, UV index, pressure, visibility, and sunrise/sunset
- Save multiple locations and swipe between them, or use your current location automatically
- Home Screen widgets in small, medium, and large sizes

### Pick your look

Seven themes, from a clean serif look to a Comic Dark skin to Condition-Based, which switches automatically with the live weather. Your theme carries across the app and every widget.

### One-time unlock, no subscriptions

My Daily Weather is free to use. A single one-time Premium purchase unlocks extra themes, larger widgets, more saved locations, and independent unit settings for wind, pressure, and distance — no subscriptions, no ads.

Weather data provided by Apple Weather.
