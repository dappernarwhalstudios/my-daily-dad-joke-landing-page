# Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the FAQ! If you have a question that isn't listed here, feel free to [reach out via email](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com).

---

### What is *My Daily Weather*?

It's a weather app that shows every temperature in both Celsius and Fahrenheit at once, so you never have to switch units or do the math yourself.

---

### How do I add a location?

Tap the locations icon, then the `+` button to search for a city. Free accounts can save up to 2 locations; Premium unlocks unlimited locations.

---

### How do I switch between my saved locations?

Swipe left or right on the forecast screen, or open the locations list and tap any saved city.

---

### Can I use my current location?

Yes — grant location access when prompted, and "Current Location" will always show the forecast for wherever you are.

---

### Is there a widget?

Yes! Add the *My Daily Weather* widget to your Home Screen in small, medium, or large size. Long-press your Home Screen, tap the `+`, and search for our widget.

---

### Can I change how the app looks?

Yes — pick from seven themes, including Condition-Based, which automatically changes to match the live weather. Your theme applies across the app and your widget.

---

### What does Premium unlock?

Extra themes, larger widgets, unlimited saved locations, and independent unit settings for wind, pressure, and distance (normally bundled with your temperature unit). It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

---

### Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use, with an optional one-time Premium unlock.

---

### Can I tip the developer?

Yes — there's an optional, one-time tip jar on the About page if you'd like to support development. It doesn't unlock anything; it's just a thank-you.

---

### Do you collect any personal data?

We collect as little as possible — no account, no name or email. Check out our [Privacy Policy](/my-daily-weather/privacypolicy/) for all the details.

---

### I'm having an issue, how can I get help?

We're here for you! Email us at [dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you quickly.
