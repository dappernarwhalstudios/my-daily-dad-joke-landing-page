# Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the FAQ! If you have a question that isn't listed here, feel free to [reach out via email](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com).

---

### What is *My Daily Wisdom*?

It's an app that delivers one genuinely worth-sharing quote to your phone every day. Whether you're after motivation, a laugh, or just a moment to think, this app has you covered.

---

### How do I get a new quote each day?

The app automatically refreshes with a new quote every day. Just open it up.

---

### What if I want more quotes?

Want more? Just swipe left to load another random quote from the archive.

---

### How do I see previous days' quotes?

Swipe right to revisit quotes from previous days.

---

### Can I save or favorite quotes?

Yes! Tap the heart icon to mark a quote as a favorite. You can view all your saved quotes anytime from the Favorites tab.

---

### Will I lose my favorites if I change phones?

Nope! Just sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favorites synced across all your devices. It's quick, secure, and helps you pick up right where you left off.

---

### Is there a widget?

Definitely! Add the *My Daily Wisdom* widget to your home screen so you never miss a quote. Just long-press your home screen, tap the `+`, and search for our widget.

---

### Can I change how the app looks?

Yes! Themes let you give your daily quote a whole new look, from a clean classic style to a hand-drawn chalkboard to a newsprint-style almanac page. Pick one from the menu, and it applies across the app *and* your widget, no separate widget setup needed.

---

### Can I choose what kind of quotes I get?

Yep! Categories let you pick the kind of quotes you want — Life, Wisdom, Faith, Love, Change, Success, Perseverance, Work, Courage, and Happiness — and you can mix more than one at once for extra variety. Each category shows you how many quotes it has before you pick it, and once you've seen everything new in a category, the app loops back through old favorites instead of leaving you with nothing.

---

### Can I share quotes with friends?

Absolutely! You can share your favorite quotes via text, social media, or any app that supports sharing.

---

### Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. There's also an optional one-time purchase to remove ads and support development.

---

### What if I don't want to see ads?

You can make a small in-app purchase to remove ads permanently and enjoy a cleaner experience.

---

### I'm having an issue, how can I get help?

We're here for you! Email us at [dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you quickly.

---

### Do you collect any personal data?

Nope. We respect your privacy. Check out our [Privacy Policy](/my-daily-wisdom/privacypolicy/) for all the details.
