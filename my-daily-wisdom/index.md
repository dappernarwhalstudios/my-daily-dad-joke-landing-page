### What you get

- A new quote every day, picked just for you
- Swipe back through past quotes, or shuffle up something random
- Save your favorites so the good ones are never lost
- Share a quote with one tap
- A home screen widget that matches whichever theme you've picked

### Pick your look

Choose from themes ranging from clean and classic to a hand-drawn chalkboard to a newsprint-style almanac page. Change your look anytime from the menu — your widget picks up the theme too.

### Pick your topic

Not every day calls for the same kind of quote. Choose from categories — Life, Wisdom, Faith, Love, Change, Success, Perseverance, Work, Courage, and Happiness — and mix as many as you like. Every category shows you its quote count up front, so there are no surprises, and once you've worked through what's new, the app loops back through old favorites instead of just stopping.

Whether you need a nudge to get through the day, something worth sending to a friend, or just a moment to think — My Daily Wisdom has you covered, one quote at a time.
