# Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the FAQ! If you have a question that isn't listed here, feel free to [reach out via email](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com).

---

### What is *My Daily Fact*?

It's a light-hearted app that delivers a new interesting fact to your phone every day. Whether you're into animals, science, history, or just enjoy learning something new, this app has you covered.

---

### How do I get a new fact each day?

The app automatically refreshes with a new fact every day. Just open it up and start learning!

---

### What if I want more facts?

Want to keep learning? Just swipe left to load another random fact from the archive.

---

### How do I see previous days' facts?

Swipe right to revisit facts from previous days.

---

### Can I save or favorite facts?

Yes! Tap the heart icon to mark a fact as a favorite. You can view all your saved facts anytime from the Favorites tab.

---

### Will I lose my favorites if I change phones?

Nope! Just sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favorites synced across all your devices. It's quick, secure, and helps you pick up right where you left off.

---

### Is there a widget?

Definitely! Add the *My Daily Fact* widget to your home screen so you never miss a fact. Just long-press your home screen, tap the `+`, and search for our widget.

---

### Can I change how the app looks?

Yes! Themes let you give your daily fact a whole new look, from a clean classic style to a hand-drawn chalkboard to a newsprint-style almanac page. Pick one from the menu, and it applies across the app *and* your widget, no separate widget setup needed.

---

### Can I choose what kind of facts I get?

Yep! Categories let you pick the kind of facts you want, Animals, Human Body, Geography, Food, Language, Pop Culture, Money, Science, Government, and History, and you can mix more than one at once for extra variety. Each category shows you how many facts it has before you pick it, and once you've seen everything new in a category, the app loops back through old favorites instead of leaving you with nothing.

---

### Can I share facts with friends?

Absolutely! You can share your favorite facts via text, social media, or any app that supports sharing.

---

### Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. There's also an optional one-time purchase to remove ads and support development.

---

### What if I don't want to see ads?

You can make a small in-app purchase to remove ads permanently and enjoy a cleaner experience.

---

### I'm having an issue, how can I get help?

We're here for you! Email us at [dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com](mailto:dappernarwhalstudios+support@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you quickly.

---

### Do you collect any personal data?

Nope. We respect your privacy. Check out our [Privacy Policy](/my-daily-fact/privacypolicy/) for all the details.
