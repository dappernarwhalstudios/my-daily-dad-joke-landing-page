### What you get

- A new fact every day, picked just for you
- Swipe back through past facts, or shuffle up something random
- Save your favorites so the good ones are never lost
- Share a fact with one tap
- A home screen widget that matches whichever theme you've picked

### Pick your look

Choose from themes ranging from clean and classic to a hand-drawn chalkboard to a newsprint-style almanac page. Change your look anytime from the menu — your widget picks up the theme too.

### Pick your material

Not every day calls for the same kind of fact. Choose from categories — Animals, Human Body, Geography, Food, Language, Pop Culture, Money, Science, Government, and History — and mix as many as you like. Every category shows you its fact count up front, so there are no surprises, and once you've worked through what's new, the app loops back through old favorites instead of just stopping.

Whether you need something to break the ice, settle a bet, or just make someone say "wait, really?" — My Daily Fact has you covered, one fact at a time.
